What the cast does

Idle

  • Most loops use 3 to 5 images and last 0.4 to 1 s. The most common shape is 4 frames from 3 images played A B C B (11 of 43), like SOU at 200, 100, 200, 200 ms.
  • It is one drawing, nudged. In 11 of the 12 fighters I compared pixel by pixel, the head is the same pixels from frame to frame, just moved a pixel or two. Half of them never touch the boots.

Walk

  • Most walks are 4 to 6 frames (33 of 43) and 0.4 to 0.7 s a cycle. The median speed is 230 px a second.
  • In 7 of the 12 I compared, the head is the same pixels in every walk frame and only the legs and arms change. Calm walkers (KATE, FENG, ZOMA, YO) bob 2 to 3 px; brawlers like RYUKO and REMU bob 10 px or more.
  • Walking back reuses the walk's images (34 of 43), usually at a slower speed (31 of 43).

Specials

  • Few drawings, long holds. RYUKO's G BEAM uses 5 drawings in 3.75 s, with a 450 ms wind-up and one firing pose held for 2.7 s.
  • Transforms are quick. SOU's TYPE CHANGE takes 200 ms. RYUKO cuts straight from his normal pose to his armour, then swings into the firing pose through one 50 ms smear frame.
  • In the specials I checked (RYUKO, SOU, KATE, ZOMA), beams, rings, flashes and glows are separate effect objects drawn over the fighter, not part of the fighter's own frames.

Faces: the cast against round 6

  • Shape: the cast's faces are long ovals with a jaw line and a defined chin. Round 6 had a short, round baby face.
  • Size: on SOU, KATE, ZOMA, CHUNG and RYUKO the mouth sits 16 to 18 px below the eye's centre and the chin 22 to 25 px below it, with eyes 10 to 14 px tall. Round 6 was 12 and 18 px.
  • Angle: the cast turn three-quarters toward the opponent (far eye narrower, chin forward) and keep their ears mostly under their hair. Round 6 looked straight out, with a big ear showing.

What changed, and what's still open

What's new in batch 4

  • SPECIUM RAY: crouch, the lamp charges, then a thick beam about a screen long, held for 2.5 seconds (RYUKO's Getter Beam timing).
  • THREE MINUTES: a big charge, he flies off the top, and a beam falls from the sky just ahead of where he stood, then he drops back in.
  • GIANT STEP: he jumps off the top and a giant foot stamps the spot ahead. The foot is the one new drawing in this batch.
  • ULTRA SWING: SWING's grab with twice the spin, the toss, then a beam after them.
  • Effects are drawn in code as soft light, like round 7's SPECIUM beam: RING's saw ring flies forward low and slow, BARRIER holds a wall of light at his palms, RECHARGE pumps the lamp brighter and ends in a flash.

Batch 3

  • 5 specials from 14 new drawings. RING throws on RYUKO's Spinning Axe timing, SHUWATCH flies up on DoraEmon's Sky Rise, BARRIER pushes out on ALEX's REFLACT, RECHARGE powers up on ZOMA's reload.
  • SWING is RYUKO's Daisetsuzan played with the Chuck throw drawings (grab, spin, hurl), without his missile volley at the end.
  • DoraEmon and ALEX are bigger than RYUKO, so their guides and offsets were scaled to RYUKO's size first.
  • RECHARGE faces the viewer, like a power-up pose: ZOMA sits in his tank there, so SHUWA's poses were drawn from a description.

Batch 2

  • 15 attacks from 35 new drawings: punches 1 to 4 (G), kicks 1 to 4 (H), crouch punch and kick, air punch and kick, Hit Up (K), and Chuck's grab and throw (J).
  • Chops instead of punches, as the design asks: a quick knife-hand jab, rapid chops, a spinning heel kick into a somersault, a flying kick, a low sweep and a rising chop for Hit Up.
  • Each move runs RYUKO's script for it frame by frame, with his timing and his body offsets, so reach and travel match RYUKO's.
  • Where RYUKO's frame is a tank or a gun, SHUWA's pose was drawn from a description instead of RYUKO's sprite.

Still open

  • The two rapid-chop drawings came out the same, so the chop flurry shakes one drawing back and forth.
  • Air attacks, Hit Up and SHUWATCH play in place here; in the game the jump or flight carries him.
  • Batch 1 slips still open: stacked forearms in the standing guard, the chest lamp drifting in two poses, a wide landing squat.
  • Supers play in place: the screen does not shake or darken, and no opponent gets hit or thrown, since this page has no game behind it.
  • The low-life forms (red blinking lamp, the stronger SPECIUM RAY) are a colour and size change for the game build, not new art.
  • Next: put SHUWA into the game on these donor moves (the authored-fighter work in the design), plus his portraits, HUD face and palettes.

Drawings come from the OpenAI Image API (gpt-image-2 for the round 7 key drawing, gpt-image-2.6 for every pose since): one key drawing as a flat cel illustration, then image edits of it for each pose. A script turns each one into a sprite at one shared scale, matched on the head. The glows, flashes and ray are separate effect layers, like the cast's. The scripts, prompts and pose guides are in art/shuwa/ in the repo.
