# EN stock digits

`en-700.png` through `en-1000.png` are transparent, native-size pixel art for the extended EN stock display. Codex drew and recoloured them in November 2026 using the existing SCWU.KGT UI digits as reference: the zero and one silhouettes were taken from those digits, the 9 was rotated from the 6, and the 7 and 8 were assembled and edited at pixel scale. The horizontal bands use new palettes. No smoothing or anti-aliasing was applied.

These files are art only; the exporter and HUD do not yet load them.
