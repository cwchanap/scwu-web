- [ ] Four permanent native lane seats plus 1P/optional delayed ally. First enemy of each lane immediate, second and subsequent slots use native `arrive` with strict scaled below threshold. Pin source seat x (850/450 pair; 450/850 boss), reuse same x; no threshold latch (predecessor healing above mark delays arrival). Refill only with queue nonempty, after 120 complete ticks since first downed and no active KO stop, not at 119, after normal KO/reward/contact pass; no refill during result or on the exact tick 1P is KO'd by hitTest while picking/winner are still null. Both orderings of KOs in a lane and simultaneous KOs progress in logical order without overwriting an outstanding watch; absent watch when predecessor retired, no cascading on unentered enemy. Independent queues, no >6 natives. Full 34 and 36 enemy simulation via controlled damage/normal Match ticks, including native enemy-on-enemy KOs, not manually assigning completion flags.
- [ ] Pair-lane mutual bit correct when mates vs nonmates; fixed other relationships remain symmetric, including boss vs boss and player/ally; no-partner preserves lane story seat IDs and valid compact masks, summon hostility preserved after refill and summons never affect outcome or take native seat. Fresh replacement gets native stats, frozen cheat scaling and B.R. no-revive. Shared install resets per-seat state, CPU root sequence preserved across repeated index-1 swaps and a subsequent summon; old effect-owner index, caught/target/struckBy/held, score/KO, HUD target and input/history/CPU state cannot alias or strand a survivor. Test explicit idle release of a held victim with hitStop=-1 after holder direct zero-HP retirement, no global KGT stand-in removal; unrelated effects remain. Reuse same masks within 32 bits; existing MAX_FIGHTERS summon cap unchanged.
