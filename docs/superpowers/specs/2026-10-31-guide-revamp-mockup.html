Fighter: Ryuko

EX Mode

Getter pilot

All-round hero. Chains up to three from any standing punch or kick, and cancels any connected hit into his specials and supers.

Movement & basics

Move Input Description
Walk / Crouch AD/S Arrow keys also move. Hold S or ↓ to crouch.
Guard hold away Jumping attacks break a crouching guard.
Jump / Double Jump W,W in air In the air, release every direction then press jump again; steer with left or right. One double jump and one air dash per jump.
Dash / Backdash ⇧ or →→ Double-tap a direction, letting go in between, on the ground or in the air. Hold away with ⇧ to backdash.
Punch chain G→G→G Tap again when a standing punch hits or is blocked, up to three; the second punch strikes 3×. Chains can cross with the kick chain. A missed attack has no follow-up; holding a button never repeats.
Kick chain H→H→H Third standing kick strikes 8×; jumping kick strikes 3×.
Hit Up + chase K, then W on hit Upward kick. Press or hold Jump near the end of the impact pause (or just after) to chase into the air. Blocked or missed kicks can't chase.

SpecialsNo stock cost

Move Input Description
Drill Punch U or ↓←H Six hits on the ground; aerial drill while jumping — first hit lifts, each hit or block adds a weaker strike while in range. Landing ends it in recovery.
Spinning Axe I or ↓→G One axe out at a time; pressing again while it flies only makes Ryuko stand.
IORI ×3 F or ↓←G Advancing strike; press again near the end for the second, then the knockdown finisher. Holding performs one stage. Cancels into a super (2 stocks, faster form).
Throw O close Grab and throw even through guard. A miss leaves recovery; jumping avoids it, a strike interrupts startup.
Daisetsuzan J or ←↓→H Command throw: transforms, spins the opponent overhead, hurls them skyward, two missiles catch the fall. Long recovery on a miss; started far, its shorter grab stays open through it. Below 30% health becomes EVIL DST (five strikes first).
